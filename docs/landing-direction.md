# Landing direction

Mode: Persuade. Audience: shop owners checking costs on a phone after work.

## Grounded candidates

1. Da Lat shipping-label typography: origin and care, with product logistics leading.
2. Cold-room equipment catalogue: precise hardware photography and transparent specifications.
3. Ho Thi Ky price-board: direct cost comparison and locally familiar sales language.
4. Yellow shopfront tariff board: a second storefront, big plain promise, actual slot mechanism and fixed price.
5. Vietnamese retail receipt: disciplined money trail and compact transactions.
6. Apartment directory signage: kiosk locations and wayfinding lead.
7. Market opening-hours sign: seller rests while the storefront remains available.

Impeccable assigned candidate 4 (seed fce531de). User delegated design within the supplied brief.

## Direction contract

White and saturated sign-yellow, dark ink, Barlow Condensed for the compact shopfront voice, Be Vietnam Pro for readable Vietnamese. Reference: Mailchimp's committed yellow palette, interpreted as Vietnamese storefront signage rather than software branding. A neutral white ground preserves the brief's chroma-zero requirement.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a large three-line promise at left and a full-height, original 3D kiosk model at right. The cabinet visibly has flower compartments; it is explicitly labelled a conceptual model. The yellow surface occupies roughly half of the initial page and returns at the price and registration passages. No invented sales figures, testimonials or live-location claims.

Visitor path: understand the kiosk, read three actual steps, compare two exact package prices, find demo kiosk locations, try five gift questions, understand settlement and refunds, resolve doubts, register interest.

Signature interaction: choose a package in the actual price table; the registration field updates and focus moves to its section with a visible selected-package message. Reduced-motion mode uses an immediate scroll.

## Challenger judgments

Cel dawn: declined on identification and clarity; keep the discipline of foreground separation, without parallax.
Daylight section: competitive on product clarity but weaker on local shop identification; keep spatial legibility of the cabinet.
Shader portal: declined on both axes; keep total color commitment, without shaders or custom cursors.
Phosphor terminal: declined on both axes; keep explicit, readable system status in the portal.
Du Bois portraits: competitive on clarity of cost comparison; keep truthful proportional hierarchy, without decorative charts.
Botanical folio: declined on both axes and conflicts with the brief's cream/italic exclusions; keep consistent photo scale for gift choices.

Honest risk: a 3D model cannot prove manufacturing readiness; copy and caption explicitly avoid that claim. Locations are seeded demonstration locations awaiting physical verification.
