# Landing copy research

Reviewed official pages on 2026-10-08:

- VendiFlor: https://www.vendiflor.eu/ . Its heading names the florist business and flower vending machines. Its process separately describes selecting a location, installing the machine, preparing bouquets and automated selling.
- WEIMI: https://weimiflowershop.com/ . Its introduction names supplied machines and management tools, identifies the buyer groups, and separates customer purchasing, operating software and deployment.

These businesses sell equipment or franchises. FloraBot offers monthly rental of cabinet slots; their features, availability, revenue claims and commercial terms are not evidence about FloraBot.

Applied to FloraBot:

- State the service in the hero: selling flowers through a self-service cabinet with monthly slot rental.
- Explain the roles in one short flow: the shop stocks and prices flowers, the customer pays and collects, the shop tracks orders and funds.
- Use descriptive headings for rental prices, cabinet locations, reference flower photographs, shop management and consultation.
- Keep photographs in the reference collection. A flower photograph does not explain choosing a rental package or tracking orders.
- Keep prices, slot limits and demo notices visible. Put eligibility and settlement details inside labeled information disclosures.
- Keep the copy concise without removing the nouns and actions needed to understand the service.
