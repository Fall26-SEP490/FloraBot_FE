# FloraBot

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with React 19 and TypeScript for the public website and account/dashboard surfaces. Kiosk is a separate Vite application. The latest user request supersedes the original Astro brief and requires five repositories: FE, BE, Kios, AI and Mobile.

## Users

Small and medium flower shops in Ho Chi Minh City, primarily using phones after a working day. Customers buy available bouquets at a kiosk; administrators operate the network.

## Product Purpose

A second retail location: sellers rent slots by monthly subscription, stock flowers, and customers buy with VietQR. No sales commission. Funds become available after the 24-hour dispute window.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Basic package: VND 400,000/month, 3 slots. Professional: VND 1,500,000/month, 10 slots. Expired subscriptions stop sales; renewal restores eligibility. AI recommends only available bouquets and cannot control devices. PostgreSQL flow functions are the business authority.

## Brand Commitments

Vietnamese copy, candid financial terms, technical confidence. The latest user direction supersedes the original yellow storefront: bright, cute, green and white, abundant flower photography, approachable Vietnamese marketing copy. No fabricated metrics. No confirmed logo or hardware photos supplied.

## Evidence on Hand

Architecture brief dated 2026-10-07 and FloraBot_DB_v3.zip. Two seeded kiosk locations are demonstration data, not verified live retail locations. Hardware visualization must be marked as a model. No testimonials or adoption claims may be invented.

## Product Principles

Show cost and operation clearly. Preserve tenant boundaries. Treat device and AI output as untrusted. Never present demo payment as a real payment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA; keyboard navigation; visible focus; reduced motion; Vietnamese font coverage; mobile base text 18px; touch targets 44px. Automated checks do not replace NVDA/manual verification.

## Delegated Decisions

The user requested autonomous execution ('tự làm'). Visual direction and implementation details within the supplied brief are delegated. External credentials, physical hardware verification, and actual deployment remain external dependencies.
